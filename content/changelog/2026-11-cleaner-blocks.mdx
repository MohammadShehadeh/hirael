Changed:

- **Heroes 01 to 09** no longer sit on large blurred color orbs. Heroes 01,
  02, 05 and 09 keep their shader backdrops, and the others keep their beams,
  grids and shapes.
- **Smaller glows are gone** from Blog 01, Integrations 01, 04 and 05,
  Pricing 03 and Maintenance 02.
- **Glowing lines and washes** are gone. The primary-colored lines on Contact
  04, Feature 11 and Pricing 05 cards were removed, since each card already
  has a border. Testimonial 03 uses plain hairlines, and Feature 06 and Team
  02 keep their grid without the color wash.
- **Shadows follow your theme.** CTA 01, Newsletter 01, Comparison 01 and Hero
  08 use `shadow-lg` instead of hand-written shadows.
- **Corner radii stay on the Tailwind scale** in Heroes 01, 05 and 09 and
  CTAs 01 and 03.
- **Heroes 01, 04, 05 and 09** show the Hirael H mark in the nav, in your
  foreground color.
- **Hero 09**'s stats panel joins the card with a smooth concave corner on
  each side.
- **Asme, Portfolio, Prism, Rivr and USD Halo** read more plainly, without
  words like "seamless", "effortless" and "blazing".
