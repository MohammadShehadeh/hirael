The biggest batch since the Base UI split: three new components, seven new
sections, and a quiet pass over how the whole catalog handles state.

New components:

- **Address Input**: a postal address that follows the country you pick. Choose
  the United States and you get City, State and ZIP code. Choose Germany and
  the postcode moves in front of the city and the region field disappears
  entirely. Twelve countries are described out of the box, and you can add
  your own.
- **Duration Input**: hours and minutes as separate segments you can type
  through, with digits that carry into the next segment on their own. Arrows
  step, Shift and arrows step by ten, and the value stays a plain number of
  seconds.
- **Command Palette**: the search dialog the `command` primitive stops short of.
  A platform shortcut, nested pages you can back out of with Backspace,
  remembered recents, and a shortcut hint on each row.

New sections, filling out the thinnest categories:

- **Logo clouds**: Logo Cloud 2, two marquee rows running opposite ways, and
  Logo Cloud 3, a quiet single row over a pull quote.
- **Stats**: Stats 2, four tiles each with their own sparkline.
- **Comparison**: Comparison 2, a three-column feature matrix with one column
  highlighted, and Comparison 3, before and after side by side.
- **Integrations**: Integrations 2, a filterable connector grid, and
  Integrations 3, a ruled connector list beside a sticky intro.

Fixed, across the catalog:

- **No more stale frames.** A handful of components used to correct themselves
  one frame late: the audio player kept the previous track's progress for an
  instant, the lightbox opened a new image at the last one's pan offset, the
  mention field showed the previous highlight for a beat. They now settle
  before they paint.
- **Refs behave.** Components that keep an internal ref no longer look like
  they are reading it while rendering, which is what React's own lint has been
  warning about. Copy any of them into a project with those rules on and the
  file is quiet.
- **Lazy Select stops rebuilding its label cache.** Labels are folded in by
  content rather than by array identity, so passing a freshly built `options`
  array on every render no longer matters.
