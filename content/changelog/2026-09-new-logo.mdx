---
title: A new logo, drawn once
version: '6.6.0'
date: 2026-09-12
description: 'Hirael has a real mark and wordmark. The site, the favicons, the social image and the brand placeholders inside blocks all draw the same shapes now.'
---

Changed:

- **A new mark and wordmark.** The H mark is a solid glyph rather than the old
  outlined arch, and the wordmark is set beside it as a single lockup. `Logo`
  gives you the full lockup, `LogoMark` the bare H, and `LogoTile` the H on a
  raised square, so headers, sidebars and app shells pick the one that fits
  without redrawing anything.
- **One source for the shapes.** The paths live in `lib/logo-paths.ts`, which
  both the React components and the icon routes import. The favicon, the Apple
  touch icon, the PWA icon and the social image render through Satori instead of
  the DOM, so they used to carry their own copy of the artwork and drift from
  it. Now there is one copy to change.
- **Icons are simpler.** The app icons lost the inner gloss, hairline border and
  drop shadow, and the corner radius is tighter. At 32 pixels the extra layers
  read as mud; the mark alone stays legible.
- **The footer wordmark is the logo.** The oversized name behind the footer used
  to be serif text with a gradient clipped to it. It is the actual wordmark now,
  filled with the same gradient, so it matches the mark above it at every width.
- **Brand placeholders in blocks follow.** Every footer, header, app shell,
  auth screen and the agency template carried a stand-in mark of their own.
  They all use the new H, and the sample addresses scattered through members,
  settings and the shells moved to `hirael.com`.
