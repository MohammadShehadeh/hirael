The biggest release so far. Marketing pages were well covered; the screens
behind the login weren't, and the source you copied in was styled by hand in
places where shadcn/ui already had the primitive. Both are fixed.

## New components

- **Kanban**: columns of cards you can drag across, with pointer and keyboard
  moves, live announcements, and no dnd-kit.
- **Sparkline**: tiny inline SVG line, area, and bar charts with a hover
  tooltip and a reference line. Tints with `text-*`.
- **JSON Viewer**: collapsible tree with typed value colors, item counts, copy,
  and expand or collapse all.
- **Diff Viewer**: unified and split line diffs computed in the component, with
  collapsed context and a stats chip.
- **Cron Editor**: visual field builder and raw expression kept in sync, a
  human-readable preview, and the next three run times.
- **Country Select**: searchable flag picker with pinned countries, dial codes,
  and single or multiple selection.
- **Credit Card Input**: number, expiry, and CVC with brand detection, Luhn
  validation, and per-brand formatting.
- **Emoji Picker**: categories, search, skin tones, recents, and a keyboard
  grid, all self-contained.
- **Avatar Upload**: pick, crop, and zoom a profile image with a circle or
  square mask and an initials fallback.

## New blocks

- **SaaS**: Settings 1 (account, preferences, danger zone), Settings 2
  (security and notifications), Members 1 (team table with roles, invites, and
  actions), and Onboarding 1 (a four-step setup wizard on the stepper).
- **Auth**: Two-Factor Setup 1, a scan, verify, and recovery-codes flow built
  on the QR code and OTP input.
- **AI**, a new category: Prompt Input (composer with attachments, model
  select, and stop), Message Thread (streaming bubbles, tool calls, reasoning,
  and sources), and AI Chat 1, a full chat screen with history.
- **Status pages**: the 404 category now also holds Coming Soon 1 (countdown
  and notify form), Maintenance 1 (window, progress, and updates), and Error 1
  (500 with technical details).
- **Team 1** and **Stats 1**, two new marketing categories.
- **Changelog 1**, a release-notes page with a date rail and tag filter.
- **Env Editor** (cloud): a variables table with secrets, per-environment
  chips, and `.env` import.
- **Cookie Consent** (widgets): a banner with per-category switches that
  remembers the choice.

## Browse blocks in place

Block category pages no longer stop at thumbnails. Every block in a category
renders live at full size, stacked, each with its viewport and RTL controls, a
one-line install command with a package-manager picker, and links to the
source. A sticky chip row tracks where you are, so an eleven-block category is
one click to any block.

## Every item rebuilt on shadcn/ui

This is why the major version bump. The source that lands in your repo changed
shape in ways you'll notice when you read or edit it:

- **shadcn primitives everywhere.** Hand-rolled buttons, badges, labels,
  textareas, and search boxes across components, blocks, and templates are now
  `Button`, `Badge`, `Label`, `Textarea`, `InputGroup`, and `ToggleGroup`.
  Template kits (agency-landing, rivr, usd-halo, nexacore) keep their look but
  their button primitives wrap `Button`. The CLI installs the extra
  dependencies for you; expect `button`, `badge`, `field`, `input-group`, or
  `toggle-group` to be added to your `components/ui` when you add an item.
- **Forms on `Field`.** Every form (auth, contact, newsletter, settings,
  members, onboarding, cookie consent, and the demos) uses `FieldGroup`,
  `Field`, `FieldLabel`, `FieldDescription`, and `FieldError`, with `data-invalid`
  and `aria-describedby` wired. Errors and helper text look and behave the same
  in every block.
- **One source style.** Components are named arrow-function exports, props are
  `<Name>Props` interfaces, variants are `class-variance-authority`, and prop
  types that used to override native attributes by accident (`title`, `name`,
  `id`, `onError`, `onCopy`) now say so with `Omit`. Compound parts, `data-slot`
  attributes, and prop names are unchanged, so existing usage keeps working.
- **Copy Button** is now a real `Button`: `size="sm"` for labelled, `icon-xs`
  and `icon-sm` for icon-only, `variant` passes through.

## Fixes

- Emoji Picker no longer resizes as you hover different emoji names.
- Page-scale blocks (AI Chat, Settings, Members, Onboarding, status pages)
  fill the viewport instead of collapsing in the preview.
- Country Select's list drops Israel; Palestine (+970) is included.
