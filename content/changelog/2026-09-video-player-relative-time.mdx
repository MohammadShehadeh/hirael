New:

- **Video Player.** The same parts as Audio Player, for video: play, a seek bar
  with a buffered tint, time readouts, volume and full screen. Click the video
  or press Space to play, F for full screen, M to mute. Controls fade out while
  it plays and come back when you move the pointer.
- **Relative Time.** A live "3 minutes ago" label on a real `<time>` element,
  formatted for any locale. Fresh times tick every second and older ones
  refresh less often, all from one shared timer.

Improved:

- **Blur Reveal, Scroll Reveal and Text Reveal** no longer ship hidden HTML.
  Content already on screen shows right away, and content below the fold
  reveals as you reach it. All instances on a page share one observer.
- **Header 2.** The mobile menu now drops in under the bar instead of
  stretching it, so opening it doesn't shift the page.
- **ASME.** The hero video fades in and out more smoothly.
