New:

- **Feature 10.** A mixed-cell bento for a product overview. One lattice holds a
  quote with attribution, two metric tiles, a latest-drop card with its own
  action, and a three-job list. Each cell carries a different kind of content
  rather than repeating the same tile six times.
- **Feature 11.** A feature switcher. Three capabilities sit in a vertical tab
  list and the panel beside them previews whichever one you pick, from assigned
  conversations to saved replies and snooze times. Arrow keys move between the
  tabs, so it works without a mouse.

Changed:

- **Feature 1 is rebuilt.** One bordered card of stacked stripes: a centered
  intro, two alternating rows pairing copy with a media panel you swap for a
  screenshot, then three primitive cards closing on a pair of calls to action.

Fixed:

- **Right-to-left previews mirror properly.** Framed previews built on tabs,
  menus or anything else that tracks its own reading direction stayed
  left-to-right when you flipped the RTL toggle. They now follow the page.
