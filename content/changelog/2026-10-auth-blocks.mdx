New:

- **Login 04.** Email first, password second, inside one wide card. A chip
  shows the email so you can switch accounts, and a hidden username field
  lets password managers fill the right one.
- **Login 05.** GitHub, Google and Apple stacked, then email and passkey.
  Email sends a magic link.

Improved:

- **Auth blocks use your `Card` and `Alert`**, with a plain background, a sans
  heading and no entrance animation.
- **Login 01** puts the forgot-password link beside the password label, adds a
  passkey option and shows failed attempts in an `Alert`.
- **Login 02** swaps the grid background and glow for a world map in your
  theme's colors, and its form leads with Google and GitHub.
- **Sign Up 01** leads with providers, shows a password hint under the
  strength meter and replaces the terms checkbox with a line of text.
- **Forgot Password 01**, **OTP Verify 01** and **Two-Factor Setup 01** share
  the same layout, with sentence-case copy and no uppercase labels.
- **Templates follow your theme.** Agency Landing, USD Halo, Nexacore,
  Portfolio and Mindloop drop hard-coded grays and brand colors for your
  tokens, so they work in dark mode.
- **Magnetic Button** takes `variant` and `size`, so a link rendered through it
  can look like any of your buttons.

On the site:

- **Every block page lists its category.** A row under the title links to each
  block in the same category, with the current one marked and a link back to
  the grid.
- **Previews render at real size.** Mobile, tablet and desktop load at 380,
  768 and 1280 pixels and scale down to fit, with a zoom menu beside the RTL
  and theme toggles.
