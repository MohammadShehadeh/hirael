New kinds:

- **Surveys.** An NPS card whose follow-up question changes with the score, a
  four-step feedback form with a review step, a cancellation survey that offers
  a discount or a pause depending on the reason, and a one-question-per-screen
  flow with letter-key answers and a branch that skips a question. All four are
  built on shadcn's Questionnaire, so they need a current shadcn style (the
  older `new-york-v4` style doesn't have it).
- **Search.** Faceted docs search whose counts react to the other filters, a
  search field with recent searches and full keyboard control, a results page
  with type tabs and a "Did you mean" correction, and a help center search.
- **Page headers.** A detail header with copy link, archive and undoable delete,
  a tabbed header that keeps the tab in the URL, an invoice list header whose
  toolbar searches, filters, sorts and switches to a grid, and an analytics
  header whose date range swaps every metric.
- **Empty states.** A first run that creates your first project, a no-results
  state that suggests close matches, a failed load that retries, and a members
  panel you invite people from.
- **Charts.** Revenue bars you can stack or group, traffic areas with a shared
  crosshair, a channel donut linked to its ranked list, and a conversion funnel
  that shows where people drop off. Each one writes a one-line summary from its
  own numbers.
- **Chat.** A two-pane messenger, a support widget with quick replies, a team
  channel with reactions and reply threads, and a shared inbox with snooze,
  internal notes and saved replies.
- **Bento grids.** Four grids where every tile works: a deploy log that streams,
  a kanban you can move, a funnel that follows the segment you pick, and an
  audit trail you can pause.

New components:

- **Number Field.** Step buttons, arrow keys, hold to repeat, and currency,
  percent or unit formatting. It submits the raw number.
- **Segmented Control.** One choice with a sliding thumb, keyboard support that
  mirrors in RTL, and a full-width mode.
- **Autocomplete.** Free text with suggestions from a list or an async search,
  with optional inline completion.
- **Checkbox Group.** Select all with a mixed state, nested groups, limits and a
  search that narrows the list.
- **Gauge**, **Meter** and **Text Shimmer.** A radial reading with threshold
  bands, a usage bar with segments, and a moving highlight for loading labels.

Also new:

- **Framed 404 Page.** A 404 framed by side rails, with a photo, a way home and
  a band of popular pages.

Changed:

- **Every block has a real name.** "Survey 3" is now "Cancellation Survey" and
  "Chart 1" is "Revenue Bar Chart", in the sidebar, on each page and in search.
  Install names stay the same.
- **Search finds more.** Common words like price, 404, 2FA, donut, typeahead or
  inbox now lead to the right items.
- **Faster pages.** Previews load when they scroll into view, fonts from other
  pages are no longer fetched, hero images load first, build files are cached
  for a year, and headlines no longer fade in from nothing.
- **Sans figures in more places.** Date pickers, avatars, deltas, counts and
  prices moved off the monospace font. Real code and commands keep it.

Fixed:

- **Copy Button**, **Stepper** and **Rating** demos scrolled sideways on phones.
- **Auth blocks** asked the site for a page that doesn't exist.
- **Segmented Control's** thumb stretched while it moved between options of
  different widths.
