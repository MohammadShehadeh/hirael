No new components this time. This release is about getting started faster.

New:

- **[Docs](/docs).** Installation, the Registry Customizer, theming and RTL, and installing from AI editors over MCP. The pages are hands-on: live previews, a theme playground, the files an install adds and your current tokens as swatches.
- **Customize from any preview.** Every component, block and template preview has a **Customize** button that opens the Registry Customizer, so you can restyle a demo right where you're looking at it.
- **A shortcut.** Press `⌘ .` or `Ctrl .` on any page to open it.
- **Search finds docs** and can open the Registry Customizer.

Changed:

- The Customizer is now called the **Registry Customizer**. A dot on its icon tells you when previews use settings that differ from the defaults.
- Icon-only buttons in the header and preview toolbars show a tooltip with what they do.
- A **Sponsor Me** button in the header.
