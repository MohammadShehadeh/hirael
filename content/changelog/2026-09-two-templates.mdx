New:

- **Prism.** A dark, liquid-glass landing page for an AI web design agency.
  The hero plays a looping video under a headline that blurs in word by word,
  with a partners row pinned to its foot. Below it: a how-it-works statement
  over video, two alternating feature rows with looping media, a four-card
  benefits grid, a stats band on a desaturated loop, three testimonials, and a
  video call to action that carries the footer. Barlow and Instrument Serif on
  a pure-black palette.
- **AKOR.** A landing page for an intelligent-security firm. A fixed uppercase
  navbar sits over a full-screen video hero whose copy is anchored to the
  bottom corner, then the page inverts to a near-white services band with a
  numbered 2x2 card grid, drops back to black for an about split with a
  looping video beside the mission, and closes on a simple footer. Sora on a
  charcoal palette with a vivid green primary.

Both ship in the Radix UI and Base UI trees, and both use CSS-only entrances
so nothing waits on hydration.
