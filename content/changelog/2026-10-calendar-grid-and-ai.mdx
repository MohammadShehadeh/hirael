New, for scheduling:

- **Event Calendar.** Month, week, day and agenda views. Drag an event to
  another day or time, drag its bottom edge to change how long it runs, or
  click an empty slot to add one.
- **Date Time Picker.** A calendar beside hour and minute columns in one field,
  with a 12-hour clock option and minute steps.
- **Availability Editor** for weekly working hours, and **Time Slot Picker**
  for booking a meeting time.
- **Gantt Chart** with draggable, resizable bars and day, week and month zoom.

New, for data:

- **Filter Builder.** A Linear-style filter bar with option, multi-select, text,
  number and date filters, plus `applyFilters` for client-side lists.
- **Data Grid.** An editable grid on TanStack Table and TanStack Virtual:
  arrow keys, typing to edit, paste from a spreadsheet, sorting and resizable
  columns.
- **Virtual List**, **Description List**, **Comment Thread**, **Map** on
  MapLibre and **Flow Canvas** on React Flow, all following your theme in
  light and dark.

New category, **AI**: **Reasoning**, **Tool Call** with an approval step,
**Sources** with citation previews, and **Model Selector**. **Markdown**
renders AI answers and keeps them tidy while they stream.

Also new: **Responsive Dialog**, **Theme Toggle**, **Language Switcher**,
**Masked Input**, **Tree Select**, **Transfer List**, **Expandable Text**,
**PDF Viewer**, **Upload Queue**, **File Card**, a mobile **Bottom
Navigation**, **Swipe Actions** and **Pull to Refresh**, and three hooks:
**useMediaQuery**, **useDebounce** and **useCopyToClipboard**.

Changed:

- **Spinner** and **Combobox** are no longer Hirael items, because shadcn/ui
  ships both. Blocks and components that use them now install your own copy
  from shadcn instead of overwriting it.
- **Kbd** is now **Kbd Button**: only the pressable keycap, beside shadcn's own
  Kbd and KbdGroup. Blocks that show shortcuts use shadcn's Kbd directly.
- **Floating Toolbar** and **Floating Action Button** move focus with Home and
  End as well as the arrow keys. A pressed toolbar button is now styled through
  `aria-pressed`, so restyle it with `aria-pressed:` instead of `data-active`.
- **useControllableState** keeps the same setter between renders even when
  `onChange` is an inline function.
