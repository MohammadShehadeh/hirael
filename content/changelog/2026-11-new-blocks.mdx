Added:

- **[Header 04](/blocks/header/header-04)** is a minimal bar with a Menu button. It opens a full-height menu: large numbered links that rise in one after another, beside contact details and social links. Picking a link closes the menu.
- **[Careers 03](/blocks/careers/careers-03)** lists numbered working principles above open roles grouped by team, with a remote-only switch.
- **[Changelog 03](/blocks/changelog/changelog-03)** is a dense changelog grouped by month: one line per change, a filter by type, and older months that load one at a time.
- **[Blog 03](/blocks/blog/blog-03)** is an article page: author row with a copy-link button, body copy with a pull quote and a results strip, a feedback prompt and related posts.
- **[Image Gallery 03](/blocks/image-gallery/image-gallery-03)** shows one large photo above a strip of thumbnails, with buttons and arrow keys to move between them.
- **[Process 03](/blocks/process/process-03)** pairs three numbered steps with a panel that shows what each step looks like.
- **[Newsletter 03](/blocks/newsletter/newsletter-03)** puts a signup with topic checkboxes beside a list of recent issues.
