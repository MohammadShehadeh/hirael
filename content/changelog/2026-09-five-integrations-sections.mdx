The integrations category grows from three sections to eight. Each one comes in
both the Radix and Base UI versions.

- **Integrations 4**: a centered hub with six linked tiles, and small pulses
  that travel in and out along each line.
- **Integrations 5**: three tools feeding a central hub and three receiving
  from it, joined by curved paths. Good for explaining two-way sync.
- **Integrations 6**: a row of tiles hung on a shallow arc over a faded grid.
  Each tile lifts when you hover it.
- **Integrations 7**: copy and a button beside a grid of nine tiles. One tile is
  featured and moves on by itself. Hover or focus the grid to pause it, or click
  a tile to pick it.
- **Integrations 8**: the quiet option. A short heading, one row of icons and a
  link to the full list.

The pulses and the cycling tile stop for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

Also new: **Status 2**, a grid of service health cards. Each card has a status
dot, a response-time sparkline, and uptime and response figures side by side.
