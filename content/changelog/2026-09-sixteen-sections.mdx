New:

- **Header 3.** A marketing header with Product, Solutions and Resources mega
  menus. Each flyout pairs a two-column link list with a featured entry, and on
  small screens the same groups fold into a side sheet.
- **Process 2.** Four install steps. Picking one fills the connector up to it and
  swaps in a short checklist, and the section ends on a command you can copy.
- **Stats 3.** A growth story beside four metrics measured against their targets,
  with a Quarter / Year toggle.
- **Blog 2.** A post index as dated rows rather than cards, filtered by category,
  with a thumbnail that shows up on hover.
- **Changelog 2.** A public roadmap in Now, Next and Later columns. Items take
  upvotes and sort by votes or date.
- **Newsletter 2.** A waitlist that returns your place in line, an invite link to
  copy and the rewards referrals unlock.
- **E-commerce 3.** A product page with a gallery, colour swatches and a size grid
  that disables sizes sold out in the chosen colour. Quantity is capped at what is
  in stock.
- **Team 3.** A distributed team directory showing each person's local time and
  whether they are inside working hours, sortable by time zone.
- **Careers 2.** A single job posting with the hiring stages spelled out and a
  sticky apply form that takes a CV and checks what you entered.
- **Image Gallery 2.** A photo grid you can switch to rows, with captions on hover
  and a lightbox that opens at the photo you picked.
- **Logo Cloud 4.** Customer wordmarks as tabs. Each one opens the result that
  customer got, and the row moves on by itself until you choose one.
- **Pricing 5.** A usage calculator. Drag the sliders and the estimate, line items
  and volume tier update as you go, monthly or yearly.
- **Comparison 4.** A plan matrix that highlights the column you point at and can
  hide every row where the plans are the same.
- **Testimonial 5.** Customer stories that lead with a number, in a carousel with
  a counter and a segmented progress bar.
- **E-commerce 4.** A category page with filters that really filter: counts react
  to each other, chips remove a filter, and on phones it all moves into a sheet.
- **Contact 4.** A support form that shows the current reply time and suggests
  help articles as you type the subject.

Changed:

- **Feature 11 moves.** The tab indicator slides, the active summary opens
  smoothly, and each preview panel eases in row by row.
- **Sections use the sans face.** Numbers, dates and initials across the blocks
  moved from the monospace font to tabular sans figures. Real code and terminal
  output keep monospace.
- **Every section enters.** Blocks that appeared all at once now rise in on a
  short stagger, and filters, tabs and success states ease between states
  instead of snapping. All of it stops under reduced motion.
- **Controls that did nothing now work.** Sign-in forms validate and show errors,
  Add to cart updates a cart count, Checkout confirms an order, Connect toggles an
  integration, billing toggles swap prices, install commands and email addresses
  copy, notifications mark as read, tables filter by status, and the AI chat
  history opens past conversations.
- **Near-duplicates tell themselves apart.** Pricing 1 and 2, Feature 2 and 3,
  Process 1, FAQ 7 and App Shell 4 and 5 now each cover a different case.
- **Heavy decoration is lighter.** Spinning rings, pulsing glows and per-card
  canvases gave way to static accents, and decorative dots, boxed icon tiles and
  real brand names came out of the copy.

Fixed:

- **Changelog 1** could show dates a day early west of UTC.
- **OTP Verify 1** and **Two-Factor Setup 1** code inputs overflowed on phones.
- **Contact 3** ran the words of its heading together.
- **Pricing 4** and **Testimonial 3** highlights never showed.
- **Logo Cloud 1** left empty grid cells filled in, and its numbers disagreed.
- **Feature 1** and **Hero 9** promised more items than they listed.
- **Footer 3** and **Footer 4** computed the year while rendering.
